# Abstract
It is a mini memo written in the format of published paper for fun. Including these years' experiences and thoughts. I want to use this otherwise wasted time to take a look at myself and restart the blog.
# Introduction
Time goes fast. It has been a long time since my last blog. I have been more expertised in the field of bioinformatic data analysis. And have known the details of working in a research team.
I used to be interested in neural network, learning on my own and writing some simple models. However, *Scaling/Money is all you need* stops me.
Then I dived into data mining, but still an outdated or at least not so fashion method. When complaining that I was born too late to catch up with the world, I'm also wondering that if I am not sosensitive to stand between the first ones.
Through these years, I have got many skills, but the most important thing I learned are Top-down work flow and just-do-it spirit, and *market yourself*.
Along with my changes, happens the changes of the world. I open the old scripts and blogs and other coding tools, feeling that *Artificial Inteligence* has changed the world a lot.
Just like many papers, which can be distilled into several words, these mini memo seems worthless to those who have known these ideas(or maybe not for people are curious about others' stories.) But I still want to write it for these reasons:
- May help those who like to collect blogs like myself.
- You never know it untill you write it.

### Build Your Team
You can't do all the things by yourself. Though you may be able to, but it is still not worth it. You need to find your teammate. If you are all alone, you can't avoid repeating the learn-and-do loop, and waste your time on many one-time skill.
Try to be the leader of your work. It seems impossible to be a boss with no money no network no anything, but in a project that others can benefit from, you need to try to be the leader with your skills or advantages. Utilizing everything to make the project go on and finish.
